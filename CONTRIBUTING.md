# Contributing to the Three Astral Codex

Thank you for your interest in expanding the *Three Astral* universe. This repository is a collaborative effort to build a comprehensive, atmospheric, and intellectually rigorous codex.

## How to Contribute

1. **Fork the Repository**: Create your own copy of the repository to make your changes.
2. **Create a Branch**: Use a descriptive branch name (e.g., `add-character-profile-mentor`).
3. **Draft Your Content**: Ensure your writing matches the editorial, declarative, and slightly literary tone of the existing documents.
4. **Submit a Pull Request**: Provide a clear summary of your additions or modifications.

## Editorial Standards

- **Tone**: Maintain a speculative, atmospheric, and respectful tone. Avoid overly casual language or internet slang.
- **Formatting**: Use standard Markdown. Ensure headers, lists, and emphasis are used consistently.
- **Originality**: All contributions must be original writing. Do not plagiarize existing analyses or reviews.

## Review Process

All Pull Requests will be reviewed by the repository maintainers. Feedback will be provided to ensure that all additions align with the thematic and stylistic goals of the codex. We appreciate your patience and your dedication to the *Three Astral* universe.
