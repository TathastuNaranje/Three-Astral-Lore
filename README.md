# Three Astral Lore

Welcome to the collaborative codex for *Three Astral*, a speculative fiction universe exploring consciousness, identity, and the duality of existence.

This repository serves as the definitive, community-driven encyclopedia for the *Three Astral* universe. It is a living document, expanding as the narrative unfolds and as readers contribute their own interpretations, theories, and insights.

## The Premise

*Three Astral* follows the journey of a young man whose seizures are not medical anomalies, but violent transitions of consciousness. With each episode, his mind is transported between Earth and a mystical parallel planet. As the boundaries between these realities blur, he must navigate the complex themes of perception versus reality, and the true nature of the self.

## Repository Structure

- **/characters**: Detailed character studies, psychological profiles, and relationship dynamics.
- **/worlds**: Explorations of the dual realities — Earth and the parallel planet.
- **/timeline**: The canonical sequence of events across both worlds.
- **/fan-theories**: A space for readers to submit and discuss their interpretations and speculative theories.

## Join the Codex

This repository is an invitation to collaborate. Whether you are analyzing the metaphysical implications of the protagonist's journey or mapping the geography of the parallel world, your contributions are welcome.
